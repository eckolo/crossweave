Use case: stylized-concept.
Make ONE wide landscape comparison board with THREE drawings of the SAME sealing stamp for crossweave. Pale warm gray open background, three evenly spaced columns, generous space. Every stamp fully inside its column, similar diagonal pose, rounded reddish brown handle toward upper left, short neck, squat ochre round metal stamping face toward lower right, with one simple two-stroke envelope-fold mark. These defining parts matter; exact realistic proportions do not. NO other objects, no actual paper cards. The stamps should feel illustrated and slightly asymmetric, only a few broad shapes each.
Reference image 1: the RIGHTMOST seal drawing is the sparse brushwork starting point; use the object identity and sparse marks, but soften/omit its still-crisp brass rim. Do not reproduce the other two panels.
Reference image 2: the BOTTOM waterway panel is a reference for softly merging color masses only. Do not paint a tunnel, water, arches or lamp in this stamp board.
The user prefers NOT drawing the silhouette as a clear continuous contour. Explore the compatible combination of sparse broad brushwork and softly merging paint masses. A quiet, damp, wistful feeling. Keep the actual drawing radically simplified and slightly deformed. Do not merely add watercolor texture to a realistically modeled object. Do not use a Gaussian blur over a complete drawing. Do not enclose objects with contour lines, continuous sharp color boundaries, scratchy outlines or crisp all-around rims. Large areas should lose their edge into adjacent colors, with a few carefully chosen short readable marks. Avoid small dab mosaics, fine paper grain, speckles, fiber texture, fine scratches, photorealistic lighting, realistic smooth volume, metallic shine, 3D renders, decorative borders, fake UI and text beyond the specified captions. Favor generous calm paint interiors and broad gestures. All three variations must differ visibly in how shape is suggested, not only in color or exposure. Comparable subject size, camera angle, palette, overall brightness; do NOT simply fade each successive image. This is a direction study, not final assets or final resolution.
Same restrained burnt-sienna / yellow-ochre / slate-gray palette in all three. Metal is ochre paint, no bright shining edge. Wood is an earth-color mass, no woodgrain. A very simple soft shadow can join the object's shadow side to its surroundings without becoming a complete realistic cast shadow.

LEFT / ① 色面でなじませる:
Use 3 to 5 broad wet washes. The handle is a warm spreading pool, not a modeled sphere; ochre and brown overlap softly at the neck. Lose part of the outer handle edge into warm gray and let the lower metal edge merge into the same-value shadow wash. Keep only the fold mark and one short dark contact at the neck as anchors. Quiet pooled color, few visible discrete brushstrokes. The whole shape is still identifiable, with substantial untraced edges.
CENTER / ② 少数の筆で拾う:
Lay down three soft overlapping paint masses; then use about 4 generous, decisive, irregular gouache brush strokes to suggest the asymmetric handle, connection, partly visible face and fold mark. Clearly broader and more gestural than LEFT. Leave conspicuous gaps between the strokes; do not draw a ring around the face. Keep wet softened joins and calm interiors. No scratchy dry-brush halo, no fine bristle marks.
RIGHT / ③ 輪郭を途切れさせる:
More adventurous lost-and-found edges: the handle's shadow side and much of the face perimeter share the background's value and hue, so about half the perimeter is actually absent, not just softly outlined. Connect an ochre front-face patch, an off-center reddish handle patch, a small dark neck accent and the simple fold mark; viewers reconstruct the rest. Retain rich local color rather than fading the entire drawing. More airy negative space cutting into the form, but enough connected shape to recognize the stamp. No full elliptical rim anywhere.

Typography: top centered Japanese title exactly 「封緘」. Smaller subtitle exactly 「輪郭を描き切らない比較」. Place clear labels under the corresponding drawings, outside the artwork: 「① 色面でなじませる」, 「② 少数の筆で拾う」, 「③ 輪郭を途切れさせる」. Readable dark Japanese type. No quotes, no other text. Exactly one comparison board with three distinct stamp studies.
