Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE comparison board of three background painting studies for the exploration card game crossweave. This is exploratory artwork, not final game assets. A quiet Japanese illustrated storybook mood, mysterious and a little wistful, hand-painted but radically simplified. The user found earlier concept art far too realistic and detailed; commit to strong abstraction, broad shapes and playful proportion distortion, without making the atmosphere loud or comical.

Board layout: three wide landscape panels stacked vertically, same framing and subject in all three, each with a narrow clean pale warm-gray caption strip underneath. A modest top heading reads exactly 「夜潮の排水路」 with subheading 「逆流する地下水路｜描き方の比較」. Clean Japanese typography. Panel captions exactly: 「① 少数の色面」, 「② 丸く誇張した形」, 「③ にじむ色の塊」. No other text. Give each landscape enough room and place text outside the scene. Portrait canvas suited to three wide panels. This board size is only for comparison, not an implementation resolution.

Shared subject in all three: an old underground drainage tunnel at night, extending downward into the distance, with only three large nested arch shapes, a narrow stepped walkway along the LEFT wall, a single small warm maintenance lamp on the LEFT, dark blue-gray water forming a broad ribbon through the lower half. A simple pale salt stripe at the wall waterline. Tunnel interior remains dark in the distance, no outside opening or revealed harbor. Water pushes from the lower distant part toward the foreground: suggest it with only TWO broad curved pale streaks, no individual wavelets, froth particles, or splashing. This is a subtle inexplicable place, not a horror dungeon. Use the same restrained gray teal / warm brown palette and similar legible overall brightness for fair comparison. Keep the wide center calm as a future background for cards, but draw no actual game UI.

Panel 1, few large color areas: softly handmade gouache / muted print illustration, only about 7 to 10 large flat paint shapes; two-tone arch and wall masses, no brick joints, very shallow perspective, water a single broad shape with two short strokes. Smooth quiet color interiors, softened irregular edges. No granular stippling, no modeled material or small brush marks. Deliberately as simple as a strong color thumbnail, not a finished richly detailed concept painting.
Panel 2, round exaggerated shapes: a noticeably different, squat, plump storybook tunnel, thick chunky arches, broad round-edged steps, a tiny simplified oval lamp; rounded contours and deliberately bent perspective, flatter scale, at most one shadow shape per object. Almost toy-like PROPORTIONS but strictly hand painted, NOT a 3D render or sculpted clay, not realistic gradients. No brick detailing. Gentle edges and subdued atmosphere.
Panel 3, merging color masses: large softly bleeding watercolor and ink-wash pools, spacious and economical, no drawn outline around most forms, arches indicated by three dissolving dark swathes and negative space; the path stays readable from one dark/light boundary; warm lamp as one small glow with no fixture details, water as one flowing wash and two pale dragged brush strokes. Damp soft edges, no scratchy texture or patchwork of tiny dabs. Simplify structure at least as strongly as panel 1, with different treatment of contours.

Hard exclusions across all: no realistic stonework, individual bricks, pipes, railings, ropes or chains; no visible paper grain, small flecks or particles, detailed reflection, photorealistic lighting, ray tracing, material rendering, uniform black cartoon outlines, faceted polygon mesh; no people, diving suit, monster, cards, equipment items, plot symbols, port, sea overhead, watergate, or later story revelations. Do not add a decorative frame, arrows or fake UI. Do not reproduce previous D art or converge the three styles. Distinct simplified painting methods under a shared calm mood.
